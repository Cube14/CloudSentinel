The edits in `client/src/media/destinations` are silent 1280 × 720, 24 fps VP9 WebM files paired with WebP frames from the encoded reels. Shared focal points keep the curved-screen UV crop and desktop header composition aligned. Portrait headers keep the full cinema frame over a soft photographic matte. The selected reel uses one video decoder and keeps its playhead through entry and return. Offscreen media pauses, inactive sources unload, and reduced-motion and reduced-data modes use the matching posters.

The public introduction and scroll chapters reuse the Overview, Environments, Findings and AI Analyst plates, and the access route reuses Identity. The public carousel preview uses the real carousel renderer with still frames. The [access aperture](../client/src/media/access-aperture.svg) and the transition compositions are original CloudSentinel artwork.
